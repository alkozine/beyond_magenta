Beyond Magenta · research design and sample size

Which research design fits the research question?

Choose what the research question is about, then answer yes or no. Randomised studies follow a short path. For non-randomised studies every relevant feature is asked, as several can apply at once (a cut-off, a held-back group, staggered timing), and all designs that fit are listed in order of strength. All designs can also be browsed and one picked directly. Each design leads to a sample size or minimum detectable effect calculation, and the Sample size calculator tab works before a design is chosen.
